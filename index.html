Movement & strength

Four-week progression

Only progress when symptoms are stable. Short sessions count.

Gentle home strength session

Begin with one round, move slowly, and rest as needed. Because your back pain has not been assessed, treat these as options rather than a rehabilitation prescription.

1

Supported marching · 30–45 sec

Hold a sturdy chair and lift one foot at a time. Keep the movement small and comfortable.

2

Chair sit-to-stands · 5–8 reps

Stand and sit slowly, using your hands for support if needed.

3

Wall push-ups · 6–10 reps

Keep your torso comfortable as you bend and straighten your elbows.

4

Supported calf raises · 8–12 reps

Hold a chair, rise onto your toes, and lower slowly.

Back-pain safety: stop if an exercise causes sharp pain, radiating pain, or clear worsening. Seek urgent care for new loss of bladder/bowel control, numbness around the groin/saddle area, or new/worsening leg weakness. Consider a healthcare professional or physical therapist if pain persists or limits daily life.
